## Who you can transfer to
You can send a ticket to a friend by their email address. They must already have an Authentix account that is fully verified, with a confirmed email and a verified identity. If they are not eligible you will see a general message that does not say why, to protect their privacy.

## The rules
A ticket can be transferred at most two times. Transfers close 24 hours before the event starts. Tickets can only be passed to a friend, never sold or resold, and there is no price field.

## What happens to the entry code
When you transfer a ticket your old entry code stops working at once and your friend gets a new code that only they can use. Both of you get a notification and an email.

## Limits
A transferred ticket counts toward your friend's ticket limit for that event, so a transfer cannot be used to get around the limit.
