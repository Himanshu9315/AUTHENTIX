## Who can scan
Only accounts created for gate staff can use the scanner app. A normal customer account is refused.

## How scanning works
Staff sign in, choose the event and name their gate, then point the camera at a ticket code. The screen turns green with the holder's name, or red with the reason. A count shows how many people are inside.

## No connection
If the connection drops, the scanner shows an amber message and keeps retrying the same scan for up to two minutes. After that the person is asked to scan again.
