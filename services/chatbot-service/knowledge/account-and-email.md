## Confirming your email
After you sign up we email you a link to confirm your address. The link works once. Open it on any device and your email is confirmed.

## If the email does not arrive
Check your spam folder, then ask for a new link from the Verify page. You can ask for a new link about once a minute.

## If the link says it has expired
Links can only be used once and do not last forever. Request a new one from the Verify page.

## Notifications
Important updates such as booking confirmations, refunds and transfers also appear in the bell icon at the top of the page.
