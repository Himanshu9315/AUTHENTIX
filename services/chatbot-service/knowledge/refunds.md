## If a payment fails
If your card or UPI payment is declined, you are not charged. You can try again while your seats are still held.

## If you paid but the seats were lost
Very rarely, your seats can be taken by someone else in the moment between holding and paying. If that happens we refund the full amount automatically and tell you by email and notification.

## If a payment arrives late
If your payment reaches us after your booking time has ended, it is refunded automatically as well.

## Cancelled events
If an organiser cancels an event, please contact support for help with a refund.
