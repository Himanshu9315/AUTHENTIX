## How to book
Open an event, choose your seats on the map or the number of tickets, and press Book tickets. You need an account with a confirmed email and a verified identity first. Authentix then holds your seats for you while you pay.

## The booking timer
Once you press Book tickets we hold your seats for a limited time, and a countdown on the checkout page shows how long is left. If the time runs out the seats are released and you can start again. You are never charged for an order that expires.

## Paying
You can pay by card or UPI. If a payment fails you can try again, up to three attempts for one order while your seats are still held. This is a demo site, so only the test cards and test UPI ids shown on the payment page work, and no real money moves.

## After you pay
When your payment succeeds the order is confirmed, you get a confirmation email and a notification, and your tickets appear in My tickets within a few seconds.
