## What not to share
Never share your Aadhaar number, one-time codes, card details, email or phone number with this assistant. It does not need them and will decline to continue if you include them.

## What this assistant can see
It only sees the question you type and the last few messages in this chat. It cannot see your account, your tickets or your orders.

## What we keep
We keep a scrambled fingerprint of your Aadhaar and its last four digits, never the number. Chat messages are not stored.
