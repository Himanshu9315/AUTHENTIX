## What the waiting room is
For very popular events you join a short online queue before you can book. It keeps the sale fair and stops the site being overwhelmed. Events that need it show a Join the queue button.

## How it works
You join once and get a place in line. People are let in a few at a time, in the order they joined. The page shows your place, how many people are ahead and about how long you may wait. You do not need to keep pressing refresh.

## Keep the page open
You can switch to another tab for a moment, but if you close the page or stay away for more than a minute or two you lose your place and would have to join again at the back.

## When it is your turn
You are taken to the event page and have a limited time to choose and book your tickets. If that time runs out you can join the queue again.
