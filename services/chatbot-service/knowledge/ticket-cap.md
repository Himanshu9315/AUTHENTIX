## How many tickets can I buy
Each event has a limit on how many tickets one verified person can hold. The usual limit is 4 tickets per person for an event. The event page tells you if you have reached it.

## What counts toward the limit
Tickets you buy count, and so do tickets that are transferred to you. If you transfer a ticket away, that place is released from your limit.

## Why there is a limit
It stops one person from taking a large share of an event and keeps tickets available for more fans.
